# Disclaimer and trademarks

## Engineering disclaimer

crashmetrics is an engineering calculation aid. Users must independently
validate numerical results and confirm that input processing, filtering,
sampling and calculation methods match the applicable assessment
protocol. The software does not certify regulatory compliance, and its
results are not official ratings. See [Protocol notes](../protocol-notes.md)
for every interpretation the package makes.

## Sources

Formulas and limits are taken from publicly available documents:
Euro NCAP assessment and test protocols (including CP 005 and CP 007),
Bharat NCAP AIS-197, AIS-100, UN Regulations No. 94 and No. 127, UN GTR 9,
FMVSS 208, SAE J211 and the published research cited in the API
reference. No protocol text is reproduced beyond short references to
section numbers and limit values.
