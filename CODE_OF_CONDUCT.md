# Code of conduct

crashmetrics is built by and for crash safety engineers, students and
developers. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background,
employer, country or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors write in a second
  language, and many are new to Python, git or open source.
- Discuss engineering on its merits: protocols, sources, data and
  results. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give credit, cite sources, and say when something is uncertain or an
  interpretation.
- Accept review and corrections gracefully, and give them kindly.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or
  imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, or confidential data of a
  person or a company (test reports, vehicle or dummy models, internal
  documents), in issues, pull requests or files.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on others to break
  licences, non-disclosure agreements or employer policies.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, commits) and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [vsk1729](https://github.com/vsk1729), privately,
or use GitHub's "Report content" link on the comment or issue. Reports
are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content, warn,
or block anyone who does not follow this code, in proportion to what
happened.
